# Quotes under the clock

The UI shows text and author only. It alternates original site phrases with the following quotations every 60 seconds. Non-original-language versions are translations prepared for this site, not quotations from published translations.

- William Shakespeare: “We know what we are but know not what we may be.” An excerpt from Ophelia’s dialogue in Hamlet, Act 4, scene 5; not a personal declaration by Shakespeare. English original: https://www.folger.edu/explore/shakespeares-works/hamlet/read/ . Russian and Japanese are site translations.
- Anton Chekhov: “Краткость — сестра таланта.” Letter to his brother Alexander, 11 April 1889 (Old Style). Russian original: https://ru.wikisource.org/wiki/Переписка_А._П._Чехова_и_Ал._П._Чехова_(Чехов) . English and Japanese are site translations.
- Matsuo Bashō: “古池や 蛙飛び込む 水の音”. The old-pond haiku, displayed with modernized orthography. Museum source: https://idemitsu-museum.or.jp/collection/calligraphy/basho/02.php . Russian and English are site translations.

The unattributed motivational phrases are site copy and are not assigned to historical figures.
